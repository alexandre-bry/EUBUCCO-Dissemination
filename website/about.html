What is EUBUCCO?

EUBUCCO is a scientific database of individual building footprints for over 200 million buildings across the 27 European Union countries and Switzerland. It harmonizes 50 open government datasets and OpenStreetMap data to provide a common platform for urban sustainability studies.

The database focuses on three main attributes critical for climate policy: Building Type, Height, and Construction Year.

Our Cloud-Optimized Version

While the original EUBUCCO project provides data in formats like GPKG and CSV, this website provides Cloud-Optimized version of data. This was executed as part of the course GEO5019 for Master of Geomatics students from Delft University of Technology.
